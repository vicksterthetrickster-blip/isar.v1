What Isar is

Isar is a personal finance tracker that shows one net worth across accounts, currencies and countries. It is read-only: it never holds your money and has no ability to spend, transfer or withdraw anything.

Early software

Isar is in active development and currently intended for individual, non-commercial use. It is provided as-is, without warranties. Figures may be incomplete or wrong; always rely on your bank's or broker's own records for anything that matters.

Not financial advice

Nothing in Isar is a recommendation to buy, sell or do anything with your money. Exchange rates shown are daily reference rates from the European Central Bank, not rates you can transact at.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for activity under your sign-in. You can stop using Isar at any time, and "Clear saved data" removes your financial data from your account.

Fair use

Don't attempt to access other people's data, disrupt the service, or use Isar for anything unlawful.

Changes

These terms and the privacy policy will evolve as Isar grows (for example, when bank connections go live). The "last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
