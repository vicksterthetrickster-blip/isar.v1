Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026 · Plain English, kept deliberately short

The short version: Isar is a read-only net worth tracker. It never holds or moves money, it can never spend, transfer or withdraw anything, and it never sees your bank password. Your data belongs to you and is never sold or shared for advertising.

What Isar stores

Your name, email, the accounts you add, their balances and history, your investment holdings, and your settings. That's it — only what's needed to show you your net worth.

Where it's stored

In your Isar account, hosted with Supabase on servers in Europe. Access rules ensure each person can only ever read their own data. A copy is kept on your own device so the app opens instantly and works offline; signing out removes that copy.

Bank connections are read-only

When bank connections are available, they use official open-banking systems (such as PSD2 in Europe and the Consumer Data Right in Australia): you log in on your bank's own page, approve read-only access, and can revoke it at any time. Isar never receives or stores your bank credentials, and the connection cannot move money.

Services Isar relies on

Supabase (sign-in and data storage, Europe) · Vercel (hosting) · Frankfurter (European Central Bank exchange rates) · CoinGecko (crypto prices) · Logo.dev (bank logos — it receives a bank's website address, never anything about you) · Enable Banking (read-only bank connections in Europe, once enabled). None of them are given your data to use for their own purposes.

What Isar doesn't do

No selling of data. No advertising. No profiling. No financial advice — figures are for tracking only and may be imperfect; exchange rates are daily reference rates, not the rates your bank offers.

Deleting your data

"Clear saved data" inside the app deletes your financial data from your Isar account on every device. Full account deletion and data export are being built; until then, email the address below and it will be handled by hand.
